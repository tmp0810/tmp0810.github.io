This research program develops compact embedding models through **relational**, **geometric**, **spectral**, and **optimal-transport-based** knowledge distillation.

Related works include:

- **EMO:** Embedding Model Distillation via Intra-Model Relation and Optimal Transport Alignments
- **SUGAR:** Spectral and Geometry-Aware Alignment for Matryoshka Representation Distillation
- **MoL:** Mixture of Layers in Cross-Tokenizer Embedding Model Distillation
- **MIPIC:** Matryoshka Representation Learning via Self-Distilled Intra-Relational and Progressive Information Chaining
- **SAMD:** Span-Aware Matryoshka Distillation for Cross-Tokenizer Embedding Models
- **MOEMD:** Multi-Faceted Distillation via Mixture-of-Experts for Cross-Tokenizer Embedding Models

See the [Publications](/publications/) page for the complete author and venue information.
