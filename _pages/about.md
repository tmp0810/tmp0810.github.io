Hi, I'm **Minh-Phuc Truong**, a final-year B.Sc. student in Data Science and AI at [Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://www.hust.edu.vn/).

My research focuses on efficient and low-resource machine learning, with particular interests in **optimal transport**, **representation learning**, **model distillation**, **optimization**, and the **efficient adaptation of foundation models**.

I am currently a Research Student at [BK.AI](https://bkai.ai/), an independent research collaborator working on Optimal Transport with [Asst. Prof. Khai Nguyen](https://khainb.github.io/) at Texas A&M University, and an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm.

### Research Interests

- Efficient Machine Learning
- Optimal Transport and Sliced Optimal Transport
- Representation Learning and Model Distillation
- Efficient Adaptation of Foundation Models

For more details, see my [research projects](/research/), [publications](/publications/), or [CV](/cv/).
