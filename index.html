Minh-Phuc Truong

Researching efficient learning, optimal transport, and representation learning

I’m a final-year Data Science and AI student at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. I’m currently an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm AI Research, working under Dr. Toan Tran on efficient adaptation of foundation models. I also conduct independent research with Asst. Prof. Khai Nguyen at Texas A&M University on Computational Optimal Transport. Previously, I was a Research Student at the International Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (BK.AI), mentored by Dr. Ngo Van Linh and Dr. Trung Le.

Research: My research interests include low-resource learning methods, as well as the theory and methodology of ML and DL for improving the efficiency and compactness of learning systems. I am especially interested in approaches such as Knowledge Distillation and Matryoshka Representation Learning, which aim to build compact yet effective representations and models. My current focus is on Computational Optimal Transport, with a particular interest in Sliced Optimal Transport. I am interested in developing scalable and computationally efficient optimal transport methods by leveraging low-dimensional projections, while also studying their theoretical properties and applications to representation learning and modern machine learning systems.

For more details, see my publications or CV.
